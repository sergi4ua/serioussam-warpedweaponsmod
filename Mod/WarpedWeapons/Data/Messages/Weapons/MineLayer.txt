WEAPON:    Energy Mine Launcher
AMMO:      Mine Packs (3 mines each)

DESCRIPTION:

Throws energy mines that stick around wherever they land. A mine arms itself as soon as it settles and goes off when a player walks into it, when it is shot or when its owner detonates it. Up to three mines can be active at once.

SECONDARY FIRE:

Detonates all of your placed mines.

NOTES:

- Mines do not tell friend from foe - do not walk into your own mines
- Monsters do not set mines off by touch - detonate them remotely
- Lure enemies over the mines and blow them up remotely
