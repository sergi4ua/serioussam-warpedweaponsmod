WEAPON:    Charged Plasma Thrower
AMMO:      Plasma Packs (50 cells each)
ROF:       ~170 bolts/min

DESCRIPTION:

An energy weapon built on a lasergun frame. Two barrels take turns firing fast plasma bolts that burst on impact and damage everything close to the point of impact.

SECONDARY FIRE:

Releases a spread of eight plasma bolts at once. Uses 5 cells per shot.

NOTES:

- Bolts burst on impact, so be careful when shooting at enemies close by
- Secondary fire is deadly at short range
